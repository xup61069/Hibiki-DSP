# Issue 103 handoff

## Objective

Add bounded multi-level undo/redo to `Vst3TimelineEditorV1`: fixed 8-entry undo and
redo stacks of published snapshots, oldest-eviction when full, redo cleared on new
commits, refusal during an active edit session, history cleared by `reset`. Gives the
future supervisor UI standard editing safety with fixed storage only.

## Acceptance

- Commit pushes previous published state; undo restores it exactly; redo re-applies.
- New commit after undo clears the redo stack; capacity eviction keeps at most 8
  entries dropping the oldest.
- undo/redo refuse during an active session; discard leaves stacks intact; reset
  clears both.
- No IPC frame, schema file, RT-thread, driver or permanent-identity change;
  single-writer control plane unchanged.
