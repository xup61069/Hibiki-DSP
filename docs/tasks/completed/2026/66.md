# Issue 66 handoff

## Objective

Fix the glob-to-regex conversion in `tools/context-pack.ps1`. Patterns such as `src/**/file.cpp`
must match both `src/file.cpp` and deeper descendants; the current replacement emits a mandatory
extra separator and misses direct children.

## Acceptance

- Preserve exact-path and single-star semantics: `*` and `?` do not cross `/`.
- Correct recursive semantics: `**/` matches zero or more directory segments, and `/**` matches
  descendants without requiring an extra separator.
- Add `-SelfTest` with bounded in-memory fixtures covering exact, single-star, recursive direct/deep
  matches, and rejection of near misses; self-test must not read or write repository files.
- Preserve context-pack output and deduplication behavior outside corrected glob matching.
- Required local gates and CI results pass.
